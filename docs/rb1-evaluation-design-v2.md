## H5 and document-form calibration

R13b compares a blind, whole-document LLM sorter against a handoff-free Decisions model sorter on identical dev documents; the separate reviewer role consumes a sorter handoff. The first 20-document pair completed 20/20 per arm with verified providers and one call each, but is a mechanics smoke, not evidence of non-inferiority. Report forced-choice quality, human routing, latency and cost for both arms; apply per-call price tiers, preserve provider-reported usage, and compare raw and corrected subclass scores. Use the conservative exact uncertainty floor and class coverage guard described in the [smoke review](rb1-r13b-smoke-review.md); tiny, concordant samples cannot establish non-inferiority.

Question set 4.0.1 improved form controls yet failed the 20b false-park bar. Calibration draw 1, after four misbuilt defects were excluded, showed no zero-leak floor in the investigated range: five of 63 defects passed at the 0.50 floor, with five of 50 intact documents parked. These balanced, partly constructed samples do not estimate production rates. Builder corrections and the 4.0.2 wording are in-sample changes; no floor or new wording is adopted on that evidence. Draw 2's paired 4.0.1/4.0.2 comparison, exclusions and prespecified decision rule are in the [smoke review](rb1-r13b-smoke-review.md); verify its actual execution and results before claiming an outcome.

Fit routing thresholds on the reserved calibration split, with error bounds, risk/coverage and per-class sample-size limits; dev exploration is not certification. Keep the original H5 held-out seal, preregister margins, multiplicity and arm choices before unsealing, and report underpowered or inconclusive comparisons as such. The historical reviewer v2 Decisions calibration is distinct from H5 sorter and form calibration; do not substitute the superseded chat-model calibration.

2. Preflight each run; verify identities, routing mode, document selection, trace sink, cost limits, expected calls and provenance. Paid launches retain the operator's initials and cap requirements. Re-price after material changes.
3. Complete and review the appropriate smoke and control gates before advancing to dev looks. Fix mechanisms before changing prompts; log every change with source commit and reports. Pair arms on identical IDs, preserve failures and report denominators.
